02Our services

Launch package: Website + AI Business Brain ($3,500 AUD, once-off). This covers:

  • a new website build, or a rebuild of your current website
  • setting up your AI Business Brain using your business information
  • 30 days of support starting on your launch date

Monthly retainer: Website care + AI business consulting ($1,999 AUD per month). This covers:

  • website support, maintenance and updates
  • cyber security checks, backups and monitoring
